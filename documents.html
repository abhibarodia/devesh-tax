Documents

What to gather before you file.

Use this checklist to make sure you have everything we need. You don't need every item — only the ones that apply to your situation.

info
How to send documents: After submitting the intake form and receiving your Customer ID, email all files to {{EMAIL}} with the subject line [Customer ID] – [Surname] – 2025 Tax Documents. We do not accept documents by WhatsApp or text.

Income slips

  • description
    T4 — Statement of Remuneration Paid
    From every employer you worked for in 2025.
  • description
    T4A — Pension, retirement, or other income
    Pension income, RESP educational assistance, self-employed commissions, and other income reported on a T4A.
  • description
    T4E — Employment Insurance benefits
    If you received EI benefits during the year.
  • description
    T5 — Investment income
    Interest, dividends, and other investment income from banks or brokerages.
  • description
    T5008 — Securities transactions
    Statement of securities transactions (stock sales, mutual funds, ETFs).
  • description
    T4FHSA — First Home Savings Account
    If you contributed to an FHSA during 2025.

Deductions & credits

  • description
    RRSP contribution receipts
    Official receipts from your financial institution for RRSP contributions.
  • description
    T2202 — Tuition and enrolment certificate
    From your college or university for eligible tuition fees.
  • description
    Donation receipts
    Official receipts from registered charities.
  • description
    Property tax bill
    If you own residential property and paid property tax in 2025.
  • description
    Rent receipts or lease (Ontario only)
    For the Ontario Trillium Benefit. You'll also need your landlord's name and address. CRA may ask for these later.

Identity & direct deposit

  • description
    SIN paper or confirmation
    A copy of your Social Insurance Number paper. Do not send your full SIN through the online form — only by email after intake.
  • description
    Void cheque or direct deposit slip
    So CRA can deposit your refund (or receive a payment) directly to your bank account.
  • description
    Previous year Notice of Assessment (if any)
    Helps confirm carry-forward amounts such as RRSP room and tuition credits.
